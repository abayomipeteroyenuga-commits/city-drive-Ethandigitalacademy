HOW TO PLAY MENU UPDATE
Added a How To Play button to the main menu with:
- W / Up: accelerate
- S / Down: brake/reverse
- A / Left: turn left
- D / Right: turn right
- Space: handbrake/drift
- Space + A/D: sharp 360-degree spin
- Goal and gameplay tips
The instructions open in a responsive modal and can be closed with the button, outside click, or Escape.
