# CITY DRIVE — Color & Camera Update

- G-Wagon starts in emerald green.
- Players can choose explicit paint colors from the Page 2 palette and Garage paint shop.
- Paint changes are persisted and only body paint materials are recolored; glass, lights, wheels and chrome stay intact.
- Camera C now cycles 5 modes including FRONT VIEW, which places the camera ahead of the car and looks back at the grille so the G-Wagon front is visible during races, turns and showcase driving.
