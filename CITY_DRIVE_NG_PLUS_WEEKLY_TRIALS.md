# CITY DRIVE — Weekly Trials & New Game+

## Weekly City Trials
A deterministic weekly contract rotates every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Four categories rotate through long-distance driving, cash earned, side jobs completed and race wins. Each completed contract awards cash and XP with a consecutive-week streak bonus. Progress is saved locally and works offline.

## New Game+
After all 20 campaign missions are completed, the player can start a new career tier. The feature preserves the player's garage, vehicle condition and upgrades, cash, XP, achievements, daily and weekly records, rank ID and lifetime counters. Only the 20-level campaign completion set restarts.

Each New Game+ tier tightens campaign mission time limits and increases campaign cash and XP rewards. Ten tiers are available. The original campaign remains unchanged at tier 0.

## Save compatibility
Existing version 2 saves receive defaults for the new fields and keep existing progression. The offline service-worker shell version is bumped so the new application can replace the cached shell.
